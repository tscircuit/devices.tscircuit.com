A narrower revision of the pushbutton for a desktop mount with only two screw posts.

## Mounting

The PCB measures **20 × 26 mm**, with **2 × Ø2.2 mm** clearance holes for M2 screws. The centre-to-centre spacing is **10 mm**. Orient the component side with the **J1 USB-C port at the bottom**. Measure right and up from the **lower-left corner of the PCB** in the component-side view. The hole centres are at X = 2.5 mm and Y = 7, 17 mm.

## Usage

Use the two holes along the side of the board to leave the centre free for the actuator. Check the revision before printing a mount. This prototype uses a 20 × 26 mm board and 10 mm between its two holes. The original pushbutton uses four holes.

## Protocol

The design retains the same normally open contact and CTS signal as the USB pushbutton. Use the **Button** device type when bringing up an assembled prototype. Read the USB modem-status signal **CTS**. An asserted signal means **Pressed** and a deasserted signal means **Released**. The contact sends its state through this signal without writing serial bytes.

With Web Serial, open at 9600 baud with flow control disabled, set DTR low, and read `(await port.getSignals()).clearToSend`. With WebUSB, issue a device-recipient vendor IN request with request `0x95`, value `0x0706`, index `0`, and length `2`. CTS is asserted when bit 0 of the first byte is clear. The console polls every 10 ms and accepts a state after 20 ms of stability.
