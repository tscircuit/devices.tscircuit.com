A microSD-backed controller designed for local video playback on the 3.5-inch ER-TFT035IPS-6 panel.

## Mounting

The PCB measures **48 × 48 mm**, with **4 × Ø2.2 mm** clearance holes for M2 screws. The centre-to-centre spacing is **40 × 30 mm**. Orient the component side with the **J1 USB-C port at the bottom**. Measure right and up from the **lower-left corner of the PCB** in the component-side view. The hole centres are at X = 4, 44 mm and Y = 9, 39 mm.

## Usage

Fit the bare panel and a microSD card, leaving access to the socket on the reverse side of the PCB. The design targets 30 full frames per second. Playback at that rate still needs firmware and an end-to-end measurement on hardware.

## Protocol

This prototype has a PCB design. USB application firmware and a device-console command set have not been published. Storage uses a four-bit SD bus. The display side uses a 16-bit i80 parallel interface with one RGB565 pixel per write strobe. D/C selects register commands or pixel data. A full 320 × 480 frame occupies 307200 bytes, so 30 fps requires 9.216 MB/s of pixel payload. The USB frame commands on the original flex display belong to a separate firmware implementation.
