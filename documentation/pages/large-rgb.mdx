A controller for the 7-inch ER-TFT070-2 bare 800 × 480 RGB panel.

## Usage

Use the PCB as a controller behind a larger desktop instrument or information display. Support the glass separately, check the contact side of the 50-pin flex, and allow clearance for the connector latch.

## Protocol

**RGB565 parallel scanout** · 800 × 480 pixels. This prototype has no published USB command set. The table describes the interface on the PCB.

| Operation | Request or signal | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start the panel | Sequence the logic supply, bias supply, reset and backlight. | Brings up the display electronics. |
| Scan pixels | Drive the most significant RGB inputs and the pixel clock during active video. | Displays pixels from the framebuffer. |
| Time the scan | Drive DE, HSYNC and VSYNC. | Defines active pixels, line boundaries and frame boundaries. |

One full framebuffer occupies 768000 bytes. The controller scans it continuously.

## Mounting

The PCB measures **54 × 54 mm**, with **4 × Ø2.2 mm** clearance holes for M2 screws. The centre-to-centre spacing is **40 × 40 mm**. Orient the component side with the **J1 USB-C port at the bottom**. Measure right and up from the **lower-left corner of the PCB** in the component-side view. The hole centres are at X = 7, 47 mm and Y = 7, 47 mm.
