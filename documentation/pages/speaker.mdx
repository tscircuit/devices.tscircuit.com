Plug the speaker into **J2**, connect the board to a computer with a USB-C data cable, and select **USB AUDIO DAC** in the computer’s sound output settings. Music, voice and notification sounds play through the speaker. The board mixes left and right into mono.

## Speaker connection

J2 accepts the **Same Sky CMS-23558N-L152B**, the 8 Ω, 1 W speaker used for the tscircuitboy advance. The 23 mm diameter speaker is 5.5 mm thick. Its 152 mm lead ends in a two-pin, 1 mm-pitch JST-SH plug. The matching board header is **BM02B-SRSS-TB**. Align the keyed housings and press the plug straight down. Allow room above J2 to remove the plug and leave an opening in the enclosure over the speaker.

| J2 pin | Speaker wire | Signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Red | Speaker + |
| 2 | Black | Speaker − |

Both wires are driven by the amplifier. Keep both isolated from enclosure ground. The speaker’s 1 W rating describes its power handling. This board limits gain for a low-power USB port.

[Speaker dimensions and cable drawing (PDF)](https://www.sameskydevices.com/product/resource/cms-23558n-l152.pdf)

## Protocol

The computer’s USB Audio Class 1 driver handles playback and identification. Use the normal audio output API in an app. The RP2350 USB Motherboard would need a USB Audio host driver to stream to this board.

| Operation | Host interface | Device behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identify | Enumerate USB devices | Reports **USB AUDIO DAC**, VID `08BB`, PID `27C4`. |
| Play audio | Select the playback output and stream PCM | Accepts 16-bit stereo at 32, 44.1 or 48 kHz. Both channels feed the speaker. |
| Set volume | System or application volume control | Adjusts playback level. |
| Mute | System mute control | Silences the speaker. |
| Suspend | USB bus suspend | Disables the amplifier. |

[USB audio interface datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/pcm2704c.pdf) · [Amplifier datasheet](https://www.diodes.com/assets/Datasheets/PAM8302A.pdf)

## Mounting

The PCB is **26 × 30 × 1.6 mm**. All components mount on top of the two-layer board. J2 reaches **4.3 mm above** the PCB before the speaker is plugged in. Four **Ø2.2 mm** holes accept M2 screws on a **20 × 20 mm** grid. With J1 at the bottom and the components facing you, the hole centres are at X = 3 and 23 mm, Y = 2.5 and 22.5 mm, measured from the lower-left corner.
