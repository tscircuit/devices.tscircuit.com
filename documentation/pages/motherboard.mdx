The motherboard runs a small program that can read and control USB devices inside a product. The included program lets a potentiometer select three drawings on a flex display.

<CadDownloads board="motherboard" />

## Usage

Supply regulated 5 V and ground at the underside power pads. Connect a potentiometer and a flex display to any two of the four downstream ports. Turn the knob to select a circle, diagonal stripe or checkerboard. The controller starts on product power and runs with the computer unplugged.

To inspect the program, connect the separate **Computer** port and select **Add device → Embedded motherboard**. The computer port powers the service interface. Keep product power connected to run the controller. Load the first firmware over the underside SWD pads. Each downstream device has a 100 mA design budget. Power and USB operation await first-article testing.

The first firmware discovers USB CDC serial devices. The standalone program handles the potentiometer and flex display. The distance sensor and contact-switch boards use different USB interfaces and need additional host drivers.

## Protocol

Open the computer service port at 115200 baud. Send one ASCII command per line ending in LF.

| Command | Purpose | Reply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | Identify the controller. | `DEVICE tscircuit motherboard 1.0.0` |
| `help` | List commands. | One line of command names. |
| `devices` | List the connected serial interfaces. | One `PORT` line per interface, followed by `OK`. |
| `status` | Read the selected drawing. | `SCENE 1`, `2` or `3`. Zero means the controller is waiting for a reading. |

## Mounting

The PCB is **46 × 46 × 1.6 mm**. Its four Ø2.2 mm holes fit M2 screws on a **40 × 10 mm** pattern. With the four downstream connectors at the top, the holes are 3 and 43 mm from the left edge, and 18 and 28 mm from the bottom edge. The modeled assembly extends **5.4 mm above the top face** at the port capacitors and **2.55 mm below the bottom face** at the processor module. Allow additional room for component tolerances, solder and the power harness.
