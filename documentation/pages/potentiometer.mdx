A 10 kΩ linear knob for setting an absolute level such as volume or brightness.

**Shaft-tip height: 20 mm above the component-side PCB surface**, with the frame seated and no knob fitted. The shaft is Ø6 mm with a 7 mm knurled section. A lid whose outer face sits 15 mm above the PCB leaves 5 mm of shaft projecting through it. [Alps Alpine dimensional drawing, No. 14](https://tech.alpsalpine.com/cms.media/product_catalog_rv_02_rk09k_rk09d_en_26d4cb95ac.pdf#page=6)

## Usage

Choose **Add device → Potentiometer**. Move the knob between its two end stops to see the percentage and raw reading. The shaft position is absolute, including after reconnecting.

## Protocol

**USB serial** · 115200 baud · DTR on. Send one ASCII command per line, ending in LF (`\n`).

| Command | Purpose | Reply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | Identify the device and firmware. | `DEVICE tscircuit potentiometer 1.0.0` |
| `help` | List supported commands. | One line of command syntax. |
| `status` | Request the current absolute knob position. | One JSON report, shown below. |
| `led on` | Turn the indicator on. | `OK led=on` |
| `led off` | Turn the indicator off. | `OK led=off` |

### Streamed report

The board streams one JSON object per line and repeats an unchanged reading about every 250 ms.

```json
{"module":"position","raw":128,"max":255,"uptime_ms":1000,"led":0}
```

The firmware averages eight samples. `raw` ranges from 0 to 255. To convert to percent, calculate `100 * raw / max`. `uptime_ms` counts milliseconds since power-up, and `led` is 1 when the indicator is on.

[Protocol implementation](https://github.com/tscircuit/devices.tscircuit.com/blob/main/firmware/potentiometer/potentiometer.ino)

## Mounting

The PCB measures **24 × 30 mm**, with **4 × Ø2.2 mm** clearance holes for M2 screws. The centre-to-centre spacing is **19 × 25 mm**. Orient the component side with the **J1 USB-C port at the bottom**. Measure right and up from the **lower-left corner of the PCB** in the component-side view. The hole centres are at X = 2.5, 21.5 mm and Y = 2.5, 27.5 mm.
