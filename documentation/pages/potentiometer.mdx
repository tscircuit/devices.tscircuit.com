A 10 kΩ linear knob for setting an absolute level such as volume or brightness.

## Mounting

The PCB measures **24 × 30 mm**, with **4 × Ø2.2 mm** clearance holes for M2 screws. The centre-to-centre spacing is **19 × 25 mm**. Orient the component side with the **J1 USB-C port at the bottom**. Measure right and up from the **lower-left corner of the PCB** in the component-side view. The hole centres are at X = 2.5, 21.5 mm and Y = 2.5, 27.5 mm.

## Usage

Choose **Add device → Potentiometer**. Move the knob between its two end stops to see the percentage and raw reading. The shaft position is absolute, including after reconnecting.

## Protocol

Open the USB serial port at 115200 baud, assert DTR, and terminate each text command with a newline. Use `id` to identify the board, `help` for the command list, and `status` to request a fresh report. `led on` and `led off` control the indicator.

The firmware averages eight samples and sends one JSON object per line. `raw` runs from 0 to 255. Convert it to percent with `100 * raw / max`. An unchanged value is repeated about every 250 ms.

```json
{"module":"position","raw":128,"max":255,"uptime_ms":1000,"led":0}
```
