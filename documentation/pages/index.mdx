Choose a board to see its 3D render, downloadable GLB and STEP assembly models, mounting drawing, and protocol. All mounting dimensions are in millimeters and refer to the PCB. Bare display panels need their own support.

To use a console-supported device, open the [device console](https://devices.tscircuit.com/) in desktop Chrome or Edge, choose **Add device**, and select the board. You can add several devices. Each card stays in the browser until you remove it and shows its connection state.

## Inputs

- [Pushbutton](./button.mdx) — A momentary pushbutton for a desktop trigger, shortcut, or push-to-talk control.
- [Toggle switch](./toggle-switch.mdx) — A maintained two-position lever switch for controls that should stay on or off when you let go.
- [Limit switch](./limit-switch.mdx) — A roller-lever switch for detecting when a moving part reaches a stop.
- [Rotary encoder](./rotary-encoder.mdx) — An endless dial that counts detents in either direction and doubles as a pushbutton.
- [Potentiometer](./potentiometer.mdx) — A 10 kΩ linear knob for setting an absolute level such as volume or brightness.
- [Distance sensor](./distance-sensor.mdx) — An optical time-of-flight sensor that measures the distance to a target in millimeters.

## Outputs

- [Buzzer](./buzzer.mdx) — A programmable-pitch piezo sounder for notes and alerts.
- [USB-C speaker](./speaker.mdx) — Play system audio through an 8 Ω plug-in speaker.
- [Flex display](./flex-display.mdx) — A USB adapter for streaming pictures or video to the 3.5-inch bare ER-TFT035IPS-6 panel.

## USB

- [RP2350 USB Motherboard](./motherboard.mdx) — Run a knob-to-display program on product power.
- [Four-port USB hub](./usb-hub.mdx) — A computer-powered four-port USB 2.0 hub for connecting multiple desktop modules.
- [USB resetter](./usb-resetter.mdx) — A three-port USB power switch that can restart a downstream device on a timer.

## Prototypes

- [Compact pushbutton](./compact-button.mdx) — A narrower revision of the pushbutton for a desktop mount with only two screw posts.
- [Temperature sensor](./temperature-sensor.mdx) — A compact board for measuring ambient temperature with a digital sensor.
- [2.8-inch IPS display](./small-ips.mdx) — A carrier for the 2.8-inch non-touch ER-TFT028A2-4 bare IPS panel.
- [5.83-inch e-paper display](./ereader.mdx) — A carrier for the black-and-white 5.83-inch ER-EPD0583-1B bare e-paper panel.
- [3.5-inch video player](./video-player.mdx) — A microSD-backed controller designed for local video playback on the 3.5-inch ER-TFT035IPS-6 panel.
- [7-inch RGB display](./large-rgb.mdx) — A controller for the 7-inch ER-TFT070-2 bare 800 × 480 RGB panel.
