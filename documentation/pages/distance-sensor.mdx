An optical time-of-flight sensor that measures the distance to a target in millimeters.

## Mounting

The PCB measures **24 × 30 mm**, with **4 × Ø2.2 mm** clearance holes for M2 screws. The centre-to-centre spacing is **19 × 25 mm**. Orient the component side with the **J1 USB-C port at the bottom**. Measure right and up from the **lower-left corner of the PCB** in the component-side view. The hole centres are at X = 2.5, 21.5 mm and Y = 2.5, 27.5 mm.

## Usage

Choose **Add device → Distance sensor**, grant USB access, and face the optical window toward the target. The card shows a live reading and 30 seconds of history. Design an opening around the sensor. The console flags readings outside 30–300 mm and leaves a gap for invalid returns.

## Protocol

The browser uses WebUSB to perform I²C transactions. At connection it probes 7-bit address `0x29` and expects model ID `0xEE` from register `0xC0`. It then configures the sensor and runs reference calibration.

For each single-shot measurement the driver starts ranging, waits for completion, reads 12 bytes at `0x14`, and clears the interrupt by writing `1` to `0x0B`. Distance is `(data[10] << 8) | data[11]` mm. The console accepts a completed measurement when `(data[0] >> 3) & 15` equals 11. Use the linked driver for the complete initialization and USB framing.
